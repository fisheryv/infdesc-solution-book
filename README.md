Solution Book for *An Infinite Descent into Pure Mathematics*
=========================================

## 👏 Welcome

Welcome to the *An Infinite Descent into Pure Mathematics Solution Book*. 

This project grew out of my self-study of CMU 15-151, for which *An Infinite Descent into Pure Mathematics* is the course textbook. I compiled this solution book gradually as I worked through the book, mainly to keep myself motivated to finish it.

> ⚠️**Warning:** 
>
> Please use this solution book responsibly and DO NOT plagiarize the answers to complete your assignments! Academic integrity is of utmost importance, and we must all adhere to it.

## 📑 Structure and Conventions

This answer collection book has the same structure to *An Infinite Descent into Pure Mathematics*.

This project preserves the same structure to the original book, but omits the main text and keeps only the exercises section of each chapter. The $\LaTeX$ source retains most of the original book's formatting and conventions. To distinguish exercises from solutions more clearly, I have added two custom environments: `mysolution` and `myproof`.

> **💡 Note:** 
>
> The exercises in main text are not yet included in this project, and will be added later.

## ✨ Contribution

Given my limited knowledge and expertise, the solutions here inevitably contain errors or gaps in rigor. Corrections of any kind are warmly welcomed — please feel free to point out any mistakes you find.
